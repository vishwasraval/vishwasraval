Profile Summary

About Me

I am a passionate academician, a software developer, and a researcher. I am interested in any research and developments that can help the unprivileged people of the society and bring change in their lives.

My research areas span Computer Vision, Data Science, Cyber Security, Machine Learning, Information Retrieval, WWW, and the भारतीय ज्ञान परंपरा (Indian Knowledge System - IKS).
